Severe Knee Osteoarthritis & Cartilage Wear

Knee arthritis occurs when protective joint cartilage gradually wears down, causing bone-on-bone friction, severe pain while walking, swelling, and bowing of knees.

Common Symptoms:

Pain climbing stairs, stiffness after resting, audible grinding sound (crepitus), difficulty standing straight.

Conservative Care

Hyaluronic acid injections, targeted quad strengthening, lifestyle modification.

Precision Surgery

Total Knee Replacement (TKR) or partial knee resurfacing with sub-mm alignment.

Lumbar Disc Herniation & Sciatica Pain

Sciatica manifests as sharp shooting pain, numbness, or weakness radiating from the lower back down the sciatic nerve into the legs due to disc prolapse.

Common Symptoms:

Sharp leg pain when sitting, tingling in feet, lower back stiffness, difficulty walking extended distances.

Non-Surgical Path

Selective nerve block injections, lumbar traction, core physiotherapy.

Micro-Spine Surgery

Minimally invasive discectomy or spinal decompression.

Complex Traumatic Fractures & Bone Dislocation

Accidents, falls, or high-impact athletic trauma can fracture long bones or crush joint surfaces, requiring immediate stabilization.

Immediate Indicators:

Severe acute pain, visible bone deformity, inability to bear weight, rapid localized swelling.

Closed Reduction

Casting, splinting, and radiological monitoring for simple fractures.

Internal Fixation (ORIF)

Titanium plates, intramedullary rods, and anatomical joint reconstruction.

Avascular Necrosis (AVN) & Hip Osteoarthritis

Loss of blood supply to the femoral head or chronic hip degeneration causes severe groin pain, leg length discrepancy, and restricted hip motion.

Common Symptoms:

Deep groin pain, limping gait, pain while squatting or sitting cross-legged, stiffness.

Early Stage Care

Bisphosphonates, core decompression, non-weight bearing therapy.

Total Hip Replacement

Anatomical ceramic hip replacement for permanent joint restoration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you prepare for your consultation and treatment.

When should I consider knee or hip replacement surgery?
Joint replacement is considered when severe pain restricts daily walking, standing, or sleep, and non-surgical treatments (physiotherapy, medications, joint injections) no longer provide adequate relief. Dr. Dharm evaluates your X-rays and lifestyle needs to recommend the right timing.
Can back pain or sciatica be cured without spine surgery?
Yes! Over 85-90% of spine and sciatica cases resolve with conservative treatment including targeted physical therapy, posture corrections, anti-inflammatory care, and selective nerve root blocks. Surgery is only recommended if severe neurological deficits or unmanaged pain persist.
